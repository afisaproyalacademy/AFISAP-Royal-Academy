14 September 2026 · Digital School Update

AFISAP Royal Academy Introduces a New Parent Portal

Afisap Royal Academy is pleased to introduce its new digital Parent Portal as part of the Academy's school management system. The portal gives parents and guardians a convenient way to stay connected with their wards' learning and school activities.

Through the Parent Portal, parents can view assignments posted for their wards, receive school announcements and class-specific posts, and access other relevant academic and school information made available through the system.

This new platform is designed to strengthen communication between the school and families while making important information easier to access from a phone, tablet or computer.

Parent Portal: www.afisaproyalacademy.com/parents/portal/

Learning Resources

Books and Study Materials Available for Learners

Afisap Royal Academy has a strong collection of learning resources and study materials. Parents and guardians are encouraged to obtain the required books and learning materials from the school so that pupils have access to the resources they need for effective study and classroom participation.

The Academy's focus on learning resources supports a prepared, organised and engaging learning experience for pupils across the different levels.

7 August 2026 · Graduation Ceremony

Mr. Amofa John Honours Our Final Graduands

Afisap Royal Academy was honoured to welcome Mr. Amofa John, a well-respected tutor at Prempeh College, to our graduation ceremony held on 7th August 2026. As a special guest, he had the distinguished honour of presenting graduation certificates to our final graduands, recognising their dedication, hard work and successful completion of an important stage in their education.

His presence and encouragement added significance to the celebration and reminded our graduates of the value of excellence, perseverance and continued learning. We sincerely appreciate Mr. Amofa John's support and contribution to making this memorable occasion a proud moment for our learners, parents, guardians and the wider school community.
