About Afisap Royal Academy

Afisap Royal Academy was established on 3rd September 2024 at Abuakwa Atwima Agogo, near Gaso Fueling Station, in the Ashanti Region of Ghana. The Academy was founded with a clear commitment to providing purposeful education and creating a supportive environment in which children can develop academically, socially and personally. From its foundation, the school has continued to strengthen its educational programmes, learning environment, facilities and wider school community.

The Academy provides learning opportunities from Crèche through Junior High School, creating a continuous pathway through important stages of childhood education. Its approach places emphasis on meaningful learning, character development, discipline, confidence, creativity and respect. The motto, Learning to Learn, reflects the belief that education should help children develop curiosity, independence and the ability to continue learning throughout life.

Afisap Royal Academy also recognises the importance of practical and engaging learning. The school continues to develop facilities and resources that support classroom education, practical activities, school transport, student welfare and a positive learning experience. Language learning includes English and French, while the Academy remains open to broader educational development.

The school values the partnership between management, teachers, parents and guardians. Through this collaboration, Afisap Royal Academy seeks to provide a safe, organised and encouraging environment where every learner can discover their abilities, build strong foundations and prepare for the future. The Academy welcomes families who share its commitment to quality education, responsible character and lifelong learning.
