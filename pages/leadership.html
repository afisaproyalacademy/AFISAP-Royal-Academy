A Word From Our Leaders

Welcome to Afisap Royal Academy

Our leadership team is committed to creating a purposeful learning environment where every learner is supported to grow, discover their abilities and develop strong values.

Headmaster

Mr. Ansah–Perby Owusu

Welcome Message

It is a great pleasure to welcome you to Afisap Royal Academy. As Headmaster, I am committed to providing educational leadership that promotes intellectual growth, discipline, creativity, confidence and strong moral values. Our Academy seeks to create a purposeful learning environment where every learner is encouraged to ask questions, develop critical thinking skills, discover their abilities and prepare meaningfully for the future. We recognise that quality education is a shared responsibility, requiring close cooperation among management, teachers, parents, guardians and learners. I warmly welcome our students, parents, guardians and every visitor to explore our school, our programmes and our educational vision. Together, let us nurture learners who are knowledgeable, responsible, confident and ready to contribute positively to society. Welcome to Afisap Royal Academy — Learning to Learn.

Proprietor

Mr. Adu Sarpong

Welcome Message

Welcome to Afisap Royal Academy. It is my honour, as Proprietor, to warmly welcome our cherished students, parents, guardians and every visitor to our school community and website. We are committed to building an institution where education is pursued with purpose, discipline, care and excellence. We believe every child deserves an environment that develops knowledge, confidence, character and responsibility. Through the dedication of our teachers, management, families and partners, we continue to strengthen the foundation on which our learners can succeed. I invite you to explore our Academy, discover our educational vision and join us in giving every child an opportunity to learn, grow and flourish. You are warmly welcome to Afisap Royal Academy — Learning to Learn.

Proprietress

Mad. Afia Sarpong

Welcome Message

It is my pleasure, as Proprietress, to warmly welcome our students, parents, guardians and all visitors to Afisap Royal Academy and to our school website. I believe every child deserves a safe, caring and inspiring environment in which to discover their abilities and develop a lasting love for learning. Our work combines academic development with discipline, good character, confidence and respect for others. We value a strong partnership between school and home, because parents and guardians are essential partners in every learner's educational journey. We invite you to explore our school, learn about our programmes and become part of a community dedicated to nurturing responsible, confident and capable young people. Welcome to Afisap Royal Academy — Learning to Learn.

School Management · Welfare & Health

Mrs. Rosetta Graves

Welcome Message

Welcome to Afisap Royal Academy. I am pleased to welcome our students, parents, guardians and every visitor to our school community and website. My work within the school management team focuses on the care and wellbeing of our children, including their feeding, nutrition, health-related needs, safety and general welfare. I believe children learn best when they feel cared for, secure, healthy and valued. Protecting and supporting their wellbeing requires cooperation among management, teachers, parents and guardians, and I remain committed to encouraging that partnership. We invite you to explore our school and learn more about how we support the whole child. Welcome to Afisap Royal Academy — Learning to Learn.
