Admission Information

Apply to Afisap Royal Academy

Parents and guardians can review the admission guidance below and proceed to the secure AFISAP Admissions Portal to submit an application online.

Admission Guidelines

1. Complete the application carefully.

Provide accurate information about the child, parent or guardian, contact details and the class or programme being applied for.

2. Prepare the required supporting information.

Have the applicant's recent passport photograph and any school or identification documents requested in the online application ready before submission.

3. Review all details before submitting.

Check names, dates, telephone numbers, addresses and other information to ensure that the application is complete and correct.

4. Submit one complete application.

After submission, keep the application reference or confirmation details provided by the portal for future communication with the school.

5. Await official school communication.

The school will review submitted applications and contact parents or guardians where additional information, assessment, documentation or further admission steps are required.
