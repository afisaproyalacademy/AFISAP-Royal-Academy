# Afisap Royal Academy — Website V1
Professional static website starter using the school's supplied logo, seven supplied photographs, contact details and official social links.

- About Us

- Leadership
- Admissions
- Gallery

- Contact

## Important
Unknown school information is intentionally left blank/pending. Do not publish unconfirmed Vision, Mission, fees, admission requirements, GES/NaSIA status, staff lists, grading rules, etc. until the school supplies them.
