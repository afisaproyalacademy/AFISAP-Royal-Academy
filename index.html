Our Story

From Two Pupils to a Growing Academy

Afisap Royal Academy was established on 3rd September 2024 at Abuakwa Atwima Agogo, near Gaso Fueling Station, in the Ashanti Region of Ghana. The school began with only two pupils and grew to about 50 pupils by the second term. It now serves approximately 250 learners from Crèche through JHS.
